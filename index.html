Reel Factory

Nov 2026 · AI video production

A local AI studio that turns a library of raw phone clips into finished short-form videos in a creator's own style and voice.

  • Learns an edit template from any reference reel: finds every cut to the exact frame at 30fps and reads the on-screen captions with Apple's Vision text recognition.
  • Cuts labeled clips into on-beat montages (about 20 cuts at 0.65s each) from a recipe, so every clip lands once and categories never repeat back to back.
  • Uses an LLM to write on-brand captions, grounded in a "brand brain" and tone-of-voice guide built from the creator's own story and transcripts.
  • Assembles the project inside a video editing app through an automation client, exports it, and checks the export for black frames.
  • Runs as an always-on local web app. Footage never leaves the machine.

EdgeLvl

2026 · trading platform

A semi-automated trading platform on Solana and Robinhood Chain. The user picks the coin and taps greenlight. The bot watches price every second, waits for a 15% dip and buys the 4% recovery, then takes profit on a ladder. Customers own their wallets: the bot signs under a policy that allows swaps and nothing else, and that policy is tested by trying to steal from it.

Method

How I work with AI

Agents as the team

I run AI coding agents as my engineers. Each project keeps a living brief the agent reads first: what the product is, the rules, and the decisions already made.

Context over prompts

Output quality comes from what the model knows. I write the source material it works from, like Reel Factory's brand brain and voice guide, so its work sounds like the person it's for.

Guardrails before scale

Real money and real users mean real checks: dry runs priced with live quotes, wallets the software can't drain, tests on the decision logic, and automatic checks on every export.
